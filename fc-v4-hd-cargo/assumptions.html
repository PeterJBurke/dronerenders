V4 on the MOD-L 4-inch HD Cargo frame

This is a provisional placement study using the supplied MOD-L frame STL files and the actual V4 UpperLogic/LowerPower CAD meshes from November 6, 2026. Only the frame and main flight-controller stack are shown.

What is shown

HD Cargo bottom and top plates, four regular 4-inch arms, the bottom cross plate, and six simplified 25 mm frame standoffs. The V4 boards retain their 8.64 mm facing-board gap and are centred over the frame’s existing 20 × 20 mm mounting pattern.

Placement assumptions

The controller height is provisional: available meshes have 1.000 mm minimum clearance to the frame and 1.822 mm clearance to the roof plane. No carrier, grommets, screw heads, connector mating bodies or installation tolerances are included. These gaps do not establish a buildable or vibration-qualified installation. The separate Service board is unplaced and omitted.

The supplied arm STL is 3.000 mm thick despite its 3.5 mm filename. Its geometry is preserved. Native PCB mesh thickness differs from the fabrication stack, and the J20/J21 connector body models remain absent. No PCB routing or placement changed for this visualization.
