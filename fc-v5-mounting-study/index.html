V5 mounting & layout study

Work in progress. Shunt/stack placement changed. The upper header now has 16 pins. Copper rerouting and electrical checks are in progress.

  • Four M2 × 12 mm hex spacers and eight screws hold insulating edge rails together. No screw holes added to the PCBs.
  • Structural posts: 30 × 28 mm centres. Separate frame grommet interface: 20 × 20 mm centres.
  • Carrier: 36 × 34 mm. Proposed board-face gap: 12.065 mm; connector mating tolerances remain open.
  • R10 moved next to G / BAT+ / ESC+ / G power pads; stack connector moved inward.
  • Upper UART header: four groups of TX / RX / 5 V / GND, 16 contacts total.
  • 35 mm frame roof posts are checked for this assembly; existing 25 mm posts are too short.

The JST’s 12 cable circuits carry USB, boot/reset, SWD debugging, buzzer control and power. Its two grounded mounting tabs are not extra cable pins.

Native V5 placement meshes plus a parametric carrier and drawing-derived UART/stack connector models. Inherited PCB routing is obsolete and must be rebuilt. Grommets, mating plugs, cable bends, print material and production tolerances are not qualified.
