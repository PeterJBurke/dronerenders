FC board

H743 with one upper 3×12 bank and one lower 2×10 bank, both facing the same edge. Seven UARTs include GPS; camera and microSD are included. Pads and pin labels follow current CAD; obsolete routing is omitted. Electrical routing and hardware qualification remain unfinished.

31.5 × 48 mm boards; length follows the forward axis. Four rubber grommets mount the bottom PCB on 20 × 20 mm centres. Four M2 posts and 0.1 mm insulating shims give a provisional 12.1 mm facing gap. No carrier.

Upper UARTs each have one GND, one 5 V, TX and RX. The shunt sits next to the power solder pads. The six-pin service connector uses a 100 mm cable. Upper fasteners use ordinary M2 socket-head screws.
