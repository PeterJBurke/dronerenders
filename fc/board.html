FC board

Current placement: H743, seven UARTs, GPS, camera, microSD and the six-motor connector. Pads and pin labels follow current CAD; obsolete routing is omitted. Electrical routing and hardware qualification remain unfinished.

38 × 38 mm boards. Four rubber grommets mount the bottom PCB on 20 × 20 mm centres. Four M2 posts and 0.1 mm insulating shims give a provisional 12.1 mm facing gap. No carrier.

Upper UARTs each have one GND, one 5 V, TX and RX. The shunt sits next to the power solder pads. The service cable is 100 mm.
