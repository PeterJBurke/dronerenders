FC board

Direct rubber-grommet mounting is being designed. The board geometry shown predates the new mounting holes; electrical routing is being revised.

Four 20 × 20 mm mounting positions on the bottom PCB. Four small standoffs join the boards. No carrier.

Upper UARTs each have one GND, one 5 V, TX and RX. The shunt sits next to the power solder pads. The service cable is 100 mm.
